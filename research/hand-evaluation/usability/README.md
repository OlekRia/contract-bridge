# H-42 step 2 — usability test of Fermat Complete

**Question:** can Ori and Urs count Fermat Complete fast and consistently enough for the table? This is the card with the H-54 trump-quality term (`../43-h54-trump.md`).

**Pass marks**, set in H-42 before the test:
- **Speed:** 20 seconds or less per hand, on average.
- **Agreement:** the partners reach the same contract on 80% or more of the positions.

## Material

All files are generated by `eval_lab usability data/usability-s213.jsonl usability`. The source is the reserved seed 213: 3 000 deals, double-dummy solved.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `memory-card.md` | The card to count with. Read it once before you start. |
| `questions.md` | 40 positions near the lines: 12 at the notrump game line, 4 at the notrump slam line, 16 at the major-fit game line, 8 at the slam line. Each shows both hands and the vulnerability. |
| `solutions.md` | **Don't open it until both of you have finished.** The card's counts and contracts (teams and pairs) and the double-dummy result. |
| `scoresheet.csv` | One row per position and player. |

## Procedure

1. Each partner works alone, on paper. No calculator and no discussion.
2. For each position, start a timer and count. Fill in the total, uncovered suits, keycards, the teams and pairs contracts, and the seconds taken.
3. When both of you are finished, enter the sheets into `scoresheet.csv` and send it back.

## Scoring

The scoring is done by the PI, against the pre-stated pass marks.

| Measure | How it is computed | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | average seconds per position, per player | ≤ 20 s |
| Agreement | share of positions where both chose the same teams contract (pairs reported too) | ≥ 80% |
| Accuracy | share of contracts equal to the card's answer in `solutions.md` | reported |
| Card vs double dummy | how often the card's contract is the double-dummy best | reported, not a pass mark |

**If the test fails,** the slowest or least consistent step is simplified, for example a table of shape numbers or rounding, and the test is rerun on fresh positions.
